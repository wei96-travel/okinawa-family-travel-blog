Yes. In Japan, the driver must use a child seat for a child under six years old. That rule applies when you rent a car in Okinawa as well as when you drive your own vehicle.

The mistake to avoid is reserving only "one child seat" without giving the child's height and weight. Rental companies use different seat models and size limits. A seat described as suitable for a school-age child may still be too small or too large for your child.

## The short version

- Reserve the child seat when you reserve the car, not after arrival.
- Send the child's age, height and weight to the rental company.
- Ask whether the booked vehicle can hold every child seat and all passengers.
- A child turning six does not automatically fit an adult seat belt correctly.
- At pickup, check the product label, installation and harness before driving away.

## What Japanese law requires

Japan's road traffic rules require a child under six to use a child restraint. The official Okinawa travel guide gives the same reminder to international visitors and notes that rental companies may provide a seat free or charge a fee.

Age is the legal threshold, but body size still matters after the sixth birthday. Japan's National Police Agency advises continued use of an appropriate child restraint when an adult belt does not fit a child's body correctly. JAF uses about 150 cm as a practical height reference for moving out of a booster or junior seat.

Do not treat 150 cm as the specification of every rental seat. The product supplied by the rental company may have a lower maximum height. Ask for the actual size range.

## Infant, child and junior seats

Rental forms may use different English names, so match the seat to the child rather than the label alone.

| Seat type | Typical use | What to confirm |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Infant seat | A smaller baby, often rear-facing | Minimum age or weight, rear-facing instructions and vehicle compatibility |
| Child seat | A toddler or preschool child | Height and weight range, forward-facing minimums and harness setup |
| Junior or booster seat | A larger child who still does not fit the adult belt | Minimum and maximum height, whether it has a back, and belt position |

These are planning categories, not universal product limits. Follow the label and manual on the exact seat you receive.

## What to tell the rental company

Send all of this in one message or reservation note:

- number of adults and children;
- each child's age, height and weight;
- number and type of child seats needed;
- number of large suitcases and strollers;
- whether you need ISOFIX or will use a seat-belt installation.

This matters most when two or more children need seats. A compact car may have enough listed passenger positions but become uncomfortable once two wide child seats are installed. Luggage space is a separate problem.

## Can you rent a seat after arriving?

Do not plan on it. Stock can run out, and some companies may refuse to release the car when a child under six has no reserved seat and the family has not brought one.

Fees and reservation rules differ by company. Check the policy on the same rental page used for your booking. Do not apply one company's price or installation practice to every company in Okinawa.

## Six checks before leaving the rental lot

1. Match the seat category to the reservation.
2. Read the height and weight limits on the actual seat.
3. Install it in a rear seat whenever possible and follow the product instructions.
4. Confirm the seat belt or ISOFIX connectors are routed and locked correctly.
5. Put the child in the seat and check harness height, tension and twisted straps.
6. Touch the buckle before seating the child because metal parts can become very hot in Okinawa.

Japan's transport guidance warns against placing a rear-facing infant seat in front of an active passenger airbag. If anything is unclear, ask staff to show you the manual or installation points before you leave.

## Should you bring your own child seat?

Bringing your own seat gives you a familiar product and known size range. It also creates extra airport and shuttle handling.

Before choosing this option, check:

- the airline's baggage and packaging rules;
- whether the seat can be used with the booked vehicle;
- whether it uses a seat belt or ISOFIX installation;
- how you will move it with your other luggage between the terminal and rental office.

Renting is simpler when the company can confirm a suitable model. Bringing your own may be better when fit or familiarity is more important than carrying less.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a six-year-old still need a booster seat in Japan?

The legal requirement applies to children under six. A child aged six or older should still use an appropriate junior seat if the adult belt does not sit correctly across the shoulder and pelvis. Ask the rental company for a model that fits the child's actual height.

### Is a child seat included with an Okinawa rental car?

Not always. Some companies include it and others charge a fee. Reserve it and confirm the current price before payment.

### Will rental staff install the seat?

Policies differ. Even when staff help, the driver should check the final installation and the child's harness before leaving.

### Can two child seats fit in a small rental car?

Sometimes, but passenger comfort and luggage space can become a problem. Give the rental company the full passenger, seat and luggage count and ask them to confirm the vehicle class.

### Can I put a rear-facing infant seat in the front passenger seat?

Do not place a rear-facing seat in front of an active airbag. Use the rear seat and follow the seat and vehicle instructions.
